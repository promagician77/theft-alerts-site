The plan for your footage

  1. Label a few hours from your cameras. Products in hand, phones, wallets, own bags, baskets, at your camera heights. The product detector and the pose model are fine-tuned on these.
  2. Collect hard negatives on purpose. Phones going back into pockets and wallets into bags are the alerts that wear cashiers out. Training without them doubles false alerts in the table above.
  3. Fine-tune for the ceiling angle if that is what you have. From above, wrists are hidden against the body more often; adapting to that angle is measured in the table.
  4. Let the Confirm and Dismiss taps retrain the event model weekly. Each tap is a label, stored with the clip and its features.
  5. Judge every change by false alerts per camera-hour at a fixed catch rate, on store days the model never trained on.

RTSP ingest and recording, measured

A synthetic camera burns its frame number into every frame, so each test counts exactly which frames arrived, were recorded or were lost. Loopback on this machine: numbers include H.264 encode, RTSP, relay and decode, not a real camera's encoder or your network.
